Delete your account

Last updated 30 August 2026

You can delete your DCreators account and its data at any time. There are two ways to do it, and they do exactly the same thing.

From the app

  1. Open DCreators and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Settings & Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Email support@dcreators.in from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my account. We will verify that the request comes from you and delete it within seven days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal address and profile photo.
  • For creators: your display name, bio, portfolio images, and your Aadhaar, PAN and bank details.
  • Your chat messages and notifications.
  • Your product and artwork listings are removed from the shop.
  • Your sign-in is closed permanently. The email address is freed, so you can start a fresh account with it later if you want.

What we have to keep

  • Payment records, for eight years after the transaction, because Indian company and tax law requires it. Your name is removed from them.
  • Reviews you wrote, without your name, because they form part of another person's record on the platform.

One thing will stop the deletion. If you have a project or an artwork order still in progress, we will ask you to finish or cancel it first. Someone on the other side is waiting to be paid or to receive their work, and deleting mid-deal would strand them.

Questions

Write to support@dcreators.in. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.
