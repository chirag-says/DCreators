Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

DCreators is a marketplace where clients hire photographers, designers, sculptors and artisans, and where those creators sell physical artwork. This page says what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you get rid of it.

The app is operated by DCreators (India). Contact us at support@dcreators.in.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Name, email address, phone number To create your account, sign you in, and let the other side of a booking reach you.
Postal address and PIN code To deliver artwork you buy. Collected only when you place an order.
Profile photo, portfolio images, artwork photos To show your work to clients. Portfolio and artwork images are public inside the app.
Aadhaar number, PAN, bank name, IFSC code, bank account number Collected from creators only, to verify identity before approval and to pay you. Stored where only you and our review team can read them. Never shown to clients.
Project briefs, budgets, dates, and chat messages To run the booking. Messages are visible to you and the person you are booking with.
Payment amount, order reference, and status To take payment and settle it. We never see or store your card, UPI or netbanking details; those go straight to our payment gateway.
Ratings and reviews you leave To show a creator's track record. Reviews are public inside the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, call logs, or SMS. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not run advertising.

Photos on your device

When you upload a picture, Android's own photo picker opens and hands us only the images you select. The app does not ask for access to your photo library as a whole and cannot browse it.

Who else handles your data

We use four service providers. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job.

  • Supabase hosts the database, files and sign-in. It holds everything in the table above except payment credentials. Your data is stored in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region and does not leave India.
  • Cloudinary stores and serves the images you upload.
  • Cashfree Payments processes payments. Your card, UPI or netbanking details are entered on Cashfree's page and never reach us. We receive only the amount, the order reference and whether it succeeded.
  • Expo builds and updates the app. It receives crash and version information, not your account data.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it, or where it is needed to complete a booking you started, for example giving a creator the delivery address for artwork you bought.

How long we keep it

  • Payment records are kept for eight years after the transaction, because Indian company and tax law requires it. After you delete your account these records survive with your name removed.
  • Reviews you left stay, without your name attached, because they belong to the creator's record as much as to you.
  • Everything else is erased or overwritten when you delete your account.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct anything wrong, or erase it. You can also withdraw consent, and you can nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated. Write to support@dcreators.in and we will respond within thirty days.

To delete your account, open Settings in the app and tap Delete account, or follow the steps at dcreators.in/delete-account if you have already uninstalled it.

Children

DCreators is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Your sign-in session is encrypted on your device. Access to the database is restricted per account, so one user cannot read another's private data. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users and the Data Protection Board if a breach puts personal data at risk.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and, for anything significant, tell you in the app.
