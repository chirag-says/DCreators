1. Who we are

DCreators ("we", "the platform") is operated by ____________, registered at ____________, India. These terms govern your use of the DCreators mobile app.

2. What DCreators is

We are a marketplace. Clients post creative work and hire photographers, designers, sculptors and artisans; creators list physical artwork for sale. The contract for any piece of work or any artwork is between the client and the creator, not with us. We provide the place where you find each other, agree a price, and pay.

3. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Give us accurate information and keep it current.
  • You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • You may hold both a client and a creator role on one account.

4. Creators

  • Listing as a creator requires identity verification. We review Aadhaar, PAN and bank details before approving an account, and we may decline or withdraw approval.
  • Only upload work you made or have the right to sell. You keep copyright in it; you give us permission to display it inside the app so clients can find you.
  • Deliver what you agreed, by the date you agreed.
  • Artwork sold through the shop must be physical and must be shipped. Digital-only goods may not be sold through the platform.

5. Clients

  • Describe the brief honestly, including the budget and the deadline.
  • Pay the agreed price through the app. Paying a creator outside the platform to avoid our fee is a breach of these terms and leaves you with none of the protections below.
  • Review work within the agreed number of revision rounds.

6. Money

  • Prices are set by creators and shown in Indian rupees.
  • Project payments split in half: an advance before work starts, and the balance on final approval. Artwork orders split the same way, with the balance released after you confirm delivery.
  • Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments. We do not see or store your card, UPI or netbanking details.
  • DCreators charges a platform fee of ____% on each completed transaction.
  • Creators are paid out to the bank account on their profile, within ____ working days of the client approving the work.

7. Cancellations and refunds

  • Before the advance is paid, either side may walk away with nothing owed.
  • After the advance is paid, cancelling is by agreement between client and creator. The advance covers work already done.
  • Artwork may be cancelled any time before it is dispatched. Once a consignment number is entered, it is on its way and the sale stands.
  • If a creator takes an advance and delivers nothing, write to us and we will mediate and, where the facts support it, refund you.

8. Disputes

Talk to each other first, in the project chat. If that fails, write to support@dcreators.in and we will mediate. Our decision on releasing or refunding money held on the platform is final. We are not a court and we do not decide who owns what outside the platform.

9. What you may not do

  • Post work that is not yours, or that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark.
  • Post anything unlawful, obscene, hateful, or misleading.
  • Impersonate anyone, or create an account for someone else.
  • Harass another user, or use their contact details for anything other than the booking you are both part of.
  • Attempt to break, scrape or overload the platform.

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these rules, and we will withhold payouts where fraud is involved.

We provide the platform as it is. We are not responsible for the quality, timeliness or legality of work delivered by a creator, or for a client's conduct. Where the law allows us to limit our liability, it is limited to the fees we earned on the transaction in question. Nothing here excludes liability we cannot lawfully exclude.

We may change these terms; significant changes will be announced in the app. You may close your account at any time from Settings, or as described at dcreators.in/delete-account. Obligations from bookings already under way survive the closure.
